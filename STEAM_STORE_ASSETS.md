## 2. Store Copywriting

### Short Description (Max 300 characters)
> The Revenge Simulator: Bully your own Twitch chat with Looney-Tunes physics! Drop giant cartoon anvils, Doge wrecking balls, and paint buckets straight onto in-game streamer screens down a sparking virtual cable.

### Long Description (About This Game)
**Meme@Me is an asymmetrical, role-reversal Twitch meta-game.**
For years, Twitch chat has trolled the streamer. Now, the tables have turned.

You sit before a wall of 1,000 virtual television channels, each broadcasting a simulated viewer streamer trying to survive. Pick your victim, break through the spam in the 999-viewer Group Chat Simulator, and deploy physical Looney-Tunes meme destruction!

### Key Features:
- **Looney Tunes Cartoon Violence**: Crush streamer setups with giant 500-lb anvils, crash Doge wrecking balls through their webcams, and blind their UI with explosive paint splatters!
- **Real Twitch Integration**: Connect your Twitch account in 60 seconds. Viewers trigger real-time Bit cheer reactions, subscription counter-attacks, and custom meme commands.
- **Physical Screen Obstructors**: Memes bounce, spin, and physically collide with mouse cursors and active game windows.
- **Steamworks SDK**: Full Steam achievements, cloud saves, and multiplayer lobby matchmaking for community game nights.
