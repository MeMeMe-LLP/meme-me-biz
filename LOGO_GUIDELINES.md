# Meme@Me Mascot & Logo Design Guidelines

## 1. Mascot Identity: "Glitch Doge"
The official mascot of Meme@Me is **Glitch Doge** (also known as `Doge.exe`) — a bright, neon, chaotic Shiba Inu engulfed in electric cyan and magenta glitch artifacts.

### Key Visual Rules:
1. **Neon High-Contrast**: Cyan (`#22d3ee`), Fuchsia/Magenta (`#f43f5e`), Electric Purple (`#9333ea`), and Pitch Black (`#0a0612`).
2. **Glitch Displacements**: Slight horizontal RGB-channel shift (chromatic aberration) on ears and eyes to signify viral digital corruption.
3. **Thumbnail Optimization**: Maximum legibility at 32x32px and 64x64px for Twitch chat badges and YouTube/Twitch video thumbnails.

## 3. Brand Wordmark Typography
- **Primary Font**: Heavy Display / Geometric Sans (e.g. *Impact*, *Montserrat Black*, or *Space Grotesk*).
- **Secondary / Monospace**: *Courier New* or *JetBrains Mono* for system labels (`THE REVENGE SIMULATOR`, `glitch.exe`).
